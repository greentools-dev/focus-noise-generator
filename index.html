Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Brownian noise help with focus and ADHD?

Brown noise has higher power at lower frequencies, mimicking natural phenomena like heavy waterfalls or distant thunder. Research indicates that low-frequency masking reduces sensory overstimulation and auditory distractions.

Is this really generated without audio files?

Yes. The sound waves are produced by mathematical formulas (integrators and difference equations) executed in real-time by your computer's audio processor.

Will it consume battery or mobile data?

Because it streams 0 megabytes of audio over the internet, it consumes zero network data. CPU usage is negligible (<1%) due to hardware-accelerated audio synthesis.

Can I use this while listening to music or podcasts?

Yes. You can layer this ambient sound in the background at low volumes beneath lo-fi music or video calls.
